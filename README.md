# E-Commerce Sales & Profitability Analysis Dashboard

## Overview
An interactive and dynamic Excel dashboard designed to analyze e-commerce sales performance, profitability margins, and logistics efficiency. This project transforms raw transaction data into actionable business insights.

---

## Key Performance Indicators & Features
* **Calculated Metrics**: Dynamic calculations for `Profit` (`Sales` - `Cost`), `Profit Margin %`, and month extraction using Excel formulas.
* **Category Performance Analysis**: Comparative breakdown of total sales revenue versus total profitability across product categories.
* **Logistics & Shipping Margin Analysis**: Evaluation of average profit margins segmented by delivery methods (Standard, Express, Same-Day).

---

## Key Insights
1. **Category Profitability**: Electronics yields the highest total sales volume, but Clothing shows a significantly higher relative profit margin.
2. **Shipping Method Efficiency**: Standard Shipping retains the highest average profit margin, whereas Same-Day shipping incurs higher operational costs that reduce profitability.

---

## Tools & Formulas Used
* **Excel Functions**: `TEXT`, `SUM`, calculated fields within Pivot Tables.
* **Data Visualization**: Clustered Column Charts, Pie Charts, and consolidated Dashboard layout.
